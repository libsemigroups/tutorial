# Suzuki sporadic group Suz

The presentation and the subgroup generators used for the completed
enumerations come from page 97 of
[Praeger and Soicher's book](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/low-rank-representations-and-graphs-for-sporadic-groups/0695C1C506FC1FDDF0E9B91EA228DCF1).
This presentation is not the presentation given on the ATLAS website. The
remaining subgroup generators are words in the ATLAS standard generators.

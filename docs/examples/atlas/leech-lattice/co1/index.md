# Conway group Co~1~

The presentations and subgroup generators used in these enumerations come from
Section 4.34, pages 121–122, of
[Praeger and Soicher's book](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/low-rank-representations-and-graphs-for-sporadic-groups/0695C1C506FC1FDDF0E9B91EA228DCF1).
The presentations define either $2.\mathrm{Co}_1$ or
$2\times\mathrm{Co}_1$; quotienting by the displayed central involution gives
$\mathrm{Co}_1$.

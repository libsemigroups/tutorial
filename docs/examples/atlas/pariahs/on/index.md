# O'Nan group O'N

The presentation and the generators for $L_3(7):2$ come from Section 4.23,
page 101, of
[Praeger and Soicher's book](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/low-rank-representations-and-graphs-for-sporadic-groups/0695C1C506FC1FDDF0E9B91EA228DCF1).
The tidied relations are justified in
[Soicher's paper](https://academic.oup.com/blms/article/22/2/148/327923),
which also gives the generators for the other two subgroups. This presentation
is not the presentation given on the ATLAS website.

# Mathieu group M~23~

<!-- AI assistance: OpenAI Codex prepared this page from the ATLAS and pyatlas verification files. -->

The presentations and maximal subgroup data come from the
[ATLAS of Group Representations](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/v3/spor/M23/).
The claimed order is $|\mathrm{M}_{23}| = 10,200,960$.

## Presentations

The full-group enumerations use [Presentation 1](p1.md) and
[Presentation 2](p2.md). Their pages include the defining relations,
self-contained code, recorded output, and a corresponding animation showing
the number of nodes, and the percentage completeness, of the graph.
The scripts include additional relations; we do not verify that these
relations are redundant here.
